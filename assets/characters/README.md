# 반달이 · 꼬미 (Bandal & Kkomi)

Sprite sheets of 반달이 and 꼬미, the Korea National Park Service (국립공원공단)
mascots, rendered by `tools/characters/` from the turnaround artwork in the
official *Character Standards* (캐릭터 규정집, CS06 / CS07).

The characters belong to the Korea National Park Service. This repository's
MIT license does not cover them; check the owner's terms before redistributing.
